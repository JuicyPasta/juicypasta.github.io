Zepbound injection sites: where to inject, and is there a best place?

Short answer: inject Zepbound under the skin of your stomach (abdomen) or thigh. The back of the upper arm works too, but the label says another person should give that injection. Change (rotate) the spot every week: the same area is fine, but not the same spot as last time, and the KwikPen instructions add at least 2 inches from the belly button.

The planner below keeps the rotation for you: tap the site you used last and it shows the next one.

Where is the best place to inject Zepbound?

The Zepbound label doesn't name one best place. It lists the stomach (abdomen) and thigh, plus the back of the upper arm when another person gives the injection, and says "Similar exposure was achieved with subcutaneous administration of tirzepatide in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm" (exposure is how much of the medicine reaches the bloodstream). The pen instructions say: "Your healthcare provider can help you choose the injection site that is best for you."

  • Rotate: "Change (rotate) your injection site each week. You may use the same area of your body but be sure to choose a different injection site in that area."
  • Avoid: the vial instructions say "Do not inject where the skin has pits, is thickened, or has lumps" and "Do not inject where the skin is tender, bruised, scaly or hard, or into scars or damaged skin." The KwikPen instructions keep stomach injections "at least 2 inches from the belly button".
  • Under the skin only: "Do not inject ZEPBOUND into a muscle (intramuscularly) or vein (intravenously)." (Medication Guide)

What the Zepbound label says about injection sites

The Zepbound prescribing information (section 2) says: "Inject ZEPBOUND subcutaneously in the abdomen, thigh, or another person should inject in the back of the upper arm." and "Rotate injection sites with each dose." It is given "once weekly at any time of day, with or without meals."

Zepbound comes in four forms, and each has its own Instructions for Use. They describe the sites a little differently:

  • Rotate within an area: "Change (rotate) your injection site each week. You may use the same area of your body but be sure to choose a different injection site in that area." (pen and KwikPen instructions)
  • Why rotate: the vial instructions say to rotate "to reduce your risk of getting lipodystrophy (pits in skin or thickened skin) and localized cutaneous amyloidosis (skin with lumps) at the injection sites."
  • Other injected medicines: "Do not inject ZEPBOUND in the same injection site used for other medicines." (vial instructions)
  • Which site is best: "Your healthcare provider can help you choose the injection site that is best for you." (pen instructions)

How to inject Zepbound

A short summary of the steps in the Zepbound Instructions for Use. Read the full instructions for the form you have; your doctor or pharmacist can show you how.

Zepbound single-dose pen

  1. Choose your injection site (stomach or thigh, or the back of the upper arm if someone else injects).
  2. Pull off the gray base cap. Make sure the pen is locked; "Do not put the gray base cap back on – this could damage the needle. Do not touch the needle."
  3. Place the clear base on your skin, then unlock by turning the lock ring.
  4. Press and hold up to 10 seconds: "Press and hold the purple injection button for up to 10 seconds." Listen for the first click (injection started) and the second click (injection completed); the injection is complete when the gray plunger is visible.
  5. Put the used pen in an FDA-cleared sharps disposal container right away.

Zepbound vials (single-dose and multi-dose)

Vials are injected with a syringe and needle. The prescribing information says to "always use a new syringe and needle for each injection" and to use a syringe appropriate for the dose. The vial instructions go: pull off the plastic cap and wipe the rubber stopper, attach the needle to the syringe, draw the medicine up with the vial upside down, then choose your site, insert the needle, push the plunger down, and "The needle should stay in your skin for at least 5 seconds to make sure you have injected all of your dose." Don't recap the needle; put the needle and syringe in a sharps container right away.

Zepbound KwikPen

The KwikPen holds four weekly doses and uses a new pen needle each time: "Always use a new pen needle for each injection to help prevent infections and blocked needles." Its instructions say to prime before each weekly injection (release a little medicine into the air until a drop appears), then turn the dose knob until it stops, insert the needle, and push the dose knob in "until it stops then slowly count to 5 while holding the dose knob." Unscrew the used needle into a sharps container and replace the pen cap.

Nothing here is about how much to take or when to change it: your prescriber decides that, and the dose is set on the label of the Zepbound you were given.

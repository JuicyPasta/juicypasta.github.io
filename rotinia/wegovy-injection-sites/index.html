Wegovy injection sites: where to inject, and is there a best place?

Short answer: inject Wegovy under the skin of your stomach (abdomen), thigh or upper arm. The pen instructions say the lower stomach, keeping 2 inches from your belly button, the front of the thighs, or the upper arm. Inject once a week, and change (rotate) the spot each time: the same body area is fine, but not the same spot.

The planner below keeps the rotation for you: tap the site you used last and it shows the next one.

Where is the best place to inject Wegovy?

The Wegovy label doesn't name one best place. It lists three areas, the stomach (abdomen), thigh and upper arm, and says "Similar exposure was achieved with subcutaneous administration of semaglutide in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm" (exposure is how much of the medicine reaches the bloodstream). The pen instructions say: "Your healthcare provider can help you choose the injection site that is best for you."

  • Rotate: "You may inject in the same body area each week, but make sure it is not in the same spot each time."
  • Avoid: "Do not inject into an area where the skin is tender, bruised, red, or hard. Avoid injecting into areas with scars or stretch marks." On the lower stomach, "keep 2 inches away from your belly button".
  • Under the skin only: "Do not inject WEGOVY into a muscle (intramuscularly) or vein (intravenously)." (Medication Guide)
  • If injecting is hard: the pen instructions suggest changing "to a more firm injection site, such as upper leg, or upper arm".

Where the Wegovy label says to inject

The Wegovy prescribing information (section 2): "Inject WEGOVY subcutaneously in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm. The time of day and the injection site can be changed without the need for a dosage modification." and "Rotate injection sites with each dose." Wegovy injection is given "once weekly, on the same day each week, at any time of day, with or without meals." The label also says: "Similar exposure was achieved with subcutaneous administration of semaglutide in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm."

The single-dose pen instructions are more specific:

  • "You may inject into your upper leg (front of the thighs), lower stomach (keep 2 inches away from your belly button) or upper arm."
  • "Do not inject into an area where the skin is tender, bruised, red, or hard. Avoid injecting into areas with scars or stretch marks."
  • "You may inject in the same body area each week, but make sure it is not in the same spot each time."
  • The Medication Guide: "Change (rotate) your injection site with each injection. Do not use the same site for each injection."

Wegovy also comes as a single-dose syringe, whose instructions say the back of the upper arm "only with help from someone else" and "Avoid using the exact same spot as the last time between injections", and as the Wegovy FlexTouch pen (4 weekly doses): "Choose an injection site on your upper arms, stomach or upper legs. Keep a distance of 2 inches (5 cm) from your belly button."

How to inject Wegovy with the single-dose pen

The steps from the Wegovy pen Instructions for Use, shortened. Read the full instructions in your box, and ask your doctor or pharmacist to show you the first time.

  1. Prepare: wash your hands and check your pen. Don't use it if it looks used or broken, if the medicine is not clear and colorless in the pen window, or if the expiration date has passed.
  2. Choose your injection site and clean it with an alcohol swab or soap and water; let the skin dry.
  3. Remove the pen cap: "Pull the pen cap straight off your pen."
  4. Inject: "Push the pen firmly against your skin and keep applying pressure until the yellow bar has stopped moving." You will hear 2 clicks: click 1, the injection has started; click 2, the injection is ongoing. "Do not remove the pen from your skin before the yellow bar in the pen window has stopped moving."
  5. Throw away the pen: "Put the used WEGOVY pen in an FDA-cleared sharps disposal container right away after use."

If injecting is hard: the instructions suggest changing "to a more firm injection site, such as upper leg, or upper arm or consider standing up while injecting into the lower stomach." If medicine appears on the skin, keep pressing until the yellow bar has stopped moving next time.

Wegovy FlexTouch

FlexTouch uses a new needle for every injection ("Always use a new needle for each injection"), a flow check as its instructions describe, and after pressing the dose button you "count slowly to 6" with the needle in the skin. "Do not share your pen, Wegovy FlexTouch or needles with other people, even if the needle has been changed."

Nothing here is about how much to take or when to change it: your prescriber decides that, and the dose is set on the label of the Wegovy you were given.

Ozempic injection sites (semaglutide, also Wegovy)

The Ozempic prescribing information says to inject it under the skin (subcutaneously) in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm. Its Medication Guide says the same in plain words: Ozempic "is injected under the skin (subcutaneously) of your stomach (abdomen), thigh, or upper arm." Wegovy, the other semaglutide pen, lists the same three areas. More on each: Ozempic injection sites · Wegovy injection sites.

  • Stomach (abdomen): the Instructions for Use for the Wegovy pen say to "keep a distance of 2 inches (5 cm) from your belly button", and the Ozempic and Wegovy prefilled-syringe instructions say "at least 2 inches from the belly button".
  • Thigh: the Wegovy instructions say the front of the thighs ("upper leg"). One set of Wegovy pen instructions names the lower stomach for the abdomen.
  • Upper arm: the Ozempic and Wegovy prefilled-syringe instructions say the back of the upper arm, "only with help from someone else".

Mounjaro and Zepbound injection sites (tirzepatide)

The Mounjaro and Zepbound prescribing information says to inject in the abdomen or thigh, or have another person inject in the back of the upper arm. Lilly's Instructions for Use for the KwikPen put it this way: "You or another person can inject the medicine in your thigh or stomach (abdomen) at least 2 inches from the belly button. Another person should give you the injection in the back of your upper arm." More on each: Mounjaro injection sites · Zepbound injection sites.

What is the best place to inject Ozempic or Mounjaro?

None of the labels names one best site. They list the same three areas, and the prescribing information for each of the four medicines says that similar exposure (how much of the medicine reaches the bloodstream) was achieved with injections in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm. The Instructions for Use say: "Your healthcare provider can help you choose the injection site that is best for you."

So the best spot is one of those areas that you (or the person helping you) can reach comfortably, on healthy skin, and not the spot you used last time. The labels say where not to inject:

  • where the skin is tender, bruised, red or hard, and areas with scars or stretch marks (Wegovy);
  • where the skin has pits, is thickened, has lumps, or is scaly or damaged (Mounjaro and Zepbound);
  • into a muscle or a vein (Wegovy): these are injections under the skin only.

Why rotate injection sites?

Every one of these labels tells you to change the spot with each shot:

  • Ozempic Medication Guide: "Change (rotate) your injection site with each injection. Do not use the same site for each injection."
  • Wegovy, Mounjaro and Zepbound prescribing information: "Rotate injection sites with each dose."

The Mounjaro and Zepbound vial instructions give the reason: rotate "to reduce your risk of getting lipodystrophy (pits in skin or thickened skin) and localized cutaneous amyloidosis (skin with lumps) at the injection sites."

Rotating doesn't mean you must change body areas. The Wegovy pen instructions say: "You may inject in the same body area each week, but make sure it is not in the same spot used for the last injection." The Ozempic prescribing information likewise says to use a different injection site each week when injecting in the same body region.

How to rotate: a simple 8-site pattern

Split the three areas into eight sites and move to the next one with each shot, then start again:

  1. Upper-left abdomen
  2. Upper-right abdomen
  3. Lower-left abdomen
  4. Lower-right abdomen
  5. Left thigh
  6. Right thigh
  7. Left upper arm
  8. Right upper arm

Keep the abdomen sites at least 2 inches from your belly button. If you don't use your arms (for Mounjaro and Zepbound the labels say another person should inject there), it's six sites; if you only use your abdomen, the four abdomen sites still give you a different spot every time.

The planner above uses Rotinia's rule: the next site is the one you used least recently, among the sites you use. Sites you haven't used yet come first, in the order of this list. With two or more sites ticked it never suggests the site you just used.

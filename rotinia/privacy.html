Rotinia privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-22

In short: Rotinia collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no internet connections of its own. Everything you log stays on your device unless you export it yourself.

This policy covers the Rotinia app for iOS and Android ("Rotinia", "the app"), made by Jackson Darrow ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers for the app and receive no data from it. The app has no account or sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software.

Health data stays on your device

Your medications, shots, injection sites, weights, side effects, check-ins, notes, measurements, photos, intake counts and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

  • Backups the app makes are files in the app's own folder on your device.
  • Exports (CSV, backup files, the PDF report) are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them through your phone's share sheet. What happens to a file after that is up to you and the service you chose.
  • Progress photos stay in the app's folder. They are not added to your photo library.
  • Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google's device backup), if you have it on, includes the app's data like any other app's. That backup is run by Apple or Google under their terms, and you control it in your phone's settings. The app itself does not store health information in iCloud.

Apple Health and Health Connect

Health sync is off until you turn it on, and you choose what to allow.

  • Read: body weight, to show your weight history in the app. With Rotinia Pro and your permission, also dietary protein, water, fiber and energy, to fill the intake counters.
  • Write: body weight, only if you allow it, so weights you enter in Rotinia appear in Apple Health or Health Connect.

This reading and writing happens on your device, between Rotinia and the health store on the same device. Data obtained from Apple Health or Health Connect is used only to show you your own log. It is never sent off the device by the app, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app (iPhone) or in Health Connect (Android).

Retention, deletion and security

Because the data exists only on your device, you decide how long it is kept. Entries you delete move to Recently deleted for 30 days and are then erased; deleting the app erases everything. The data is protected by your phone's own storage encryption and screen lock, and you can add the app lock on top.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service of ours is involved and no push token is collected.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your phone checks your face or fingerprint. The app only learns whether the check passed; it never sees biometric data.

Purchases

Rotinia Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns from the store whether Pro is active.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal data. We have none.

Children

Rotinia is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your choices

  • Export everything at any time, free, from Settings, Your data.
  • Turn Health sync, notifications or the app lock off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app (and, if you use one, removing it from your phone's own backup). Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase.

Email

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you.

Not medical advice

Rotinia is a personal log. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Follow your prescriber's instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Rotinia support

GLP-1 shot tracker that keeps your data

Rotinia is a private log for a weekly or daily GLP-1 medication: shots, reminders, injection-site rotation, weight and side effects. The core tracker is free, with no ads and no account, and everything you enter stays on your phone.

Need help? Email dangochains@googlegroups.com with "Rotinia" in the subject. That's the developer's support address for all his apps. Please include your phone model and what you expected to happen. Don't send health details you'd rather keep private; a description of the problem is enough.
Rotinia is a personal log. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Follow your prescriber's instructions. Missed or late dose? Check your medication's instructions or ask your prescriber.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no server, so nobody else, the developer included, can see it. See the privacy policy.

How do backups work?

Rotinia writes a backup file by itself once a week and after every 10th new entry, and keeps the newest 8. You can see them in the app under Settings, Your data. On iPhone they also show in the Files app: On My iPhone, Rotinia, Backups, where you can copy one to any place you like. Before the app updates its database, it also keeps a copy of the old one.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup, or Google's backup on Android) includes Rotinia like any other app, so a new phone restored from it has your log.

How do I export my data?

Settings, Your data, Export. Choose CSV (a zip with one spreadsheet per kind of entry) or a backup file, then pick where to send it in the share sheet. Export is free and has no limit.

How do I restore a backup?

  1. Settings, Your data, Restore, then pick a backup from the list or a backup file from your phone.
  2. Check the preview: how many entries it holds and the dates it covers.
  3. Choose Merge to add it to what's on the phone (when the same entry is in both, the newer edit wins) or Replace to swap everything for the backup.

I deleted something by mistake.

Settings, Your data, Recently deleted. Deleted entries wait there for 30 days and can be put back.

How do I move to a new phone?

Restoring the new phone from your phone's own backup brings Rotinia's data along. To move by hand, export a backup file on the old phone, send it to the new one (AirDrop, email to yourself, a cloud drive), and use Restore there.

Switching from another app

Can I bring my history?

Yes, three ways, all in onboarding and later under Settings, Your data, Import:

  • Rebuild past shots. Enter your start date, how often you take it, and the amounts you were on and from when. Rotinia fills in the past shots and shows you the list before saving.
  • Weight from Apple Health or Health Connect. If your old app or your scale wrote weights there, turning on Health sync imports the full history.
  • A spreadsheet (CSV). If your old app can export one, or you kept your own, import it and tell Rotinia which column is the date, medication, amount, site, weight and note.

Most trackers don't offer an export file, so there is no one-tap import from a named app.

Using the app

I'm not getting reminders.

Rotinia asks for notification permission when you turn on your first reminder. If you declined, allow notifications for Rotinia in your phone's Settings (on Android, also allow alarms and reminders if asked), then open the app once so it can schedule them again.

Which site does it suggest?

The one you used least recently among the sites you've turned on. It's a suggestion to help you rotate: you tap the site you actually used.

What is the "Estimated amount in body" chart?

An illustration drawn from the shots you logged and published average values for the medication (the app's "How this is estimated" sheet lists the model and sources). Your body may differ. It is not for dosing decisions; talk to your doctor before changing anything.

Does Rotinia tell me what dose to take?

No. It records what you took or were prescribed. It has no dose calculator, no unit converter and no step-up schedule.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free, for good: one medication with unlimited history, reminders, site rotation, the weight log and chart with Health sync, side effects and check-ins, notes, the estimated-amount chart, the history rebuild, import, export, backups, restore and app lock. Pro is optional, as a monthly or yearly plan or a one-time lifetime purchase, with no free trial. It adds the home-screen widget, several medications, supply tracking, intake counters, measurements and photos, insights and a PDF report for your doctor.

What happens to my Pro data if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted or hidden from your exports. The Pro screens lock until you subscribe again.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

Restore: Settings in the app, Pro, Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account. Cancel a plan in your store account: on iPhone, Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play, your profile, Payments and subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

UV index and sun-safe tan timer

Solei shows the UV index where you are and estimates how long your skin can stay in the sun, with reminders to reapply sunscreen. The core app is free, with no ads and no account.

Questions and answers

Where does the UV index come from?

From the forecast of MET Norway, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, which covers the whole world. Solei takes its clear-sky UV index and lowers it a little for cloud cover. Clouds never cut the number by more than 40%, because broken cloud can let most UV through.

How is my safe sun time worked out?

From your skin type (Fitzpatrick I to VI), the UV forecast for the coming hours and the SPF you choose. The app adds up the UV dose minute by minute and stops at two thirds of the dose that typically reddens your skin type. The numbers are cautious on purpose, and they are estimates.

Why does SPF 30 not give me 30 times longer?

Most people apply a quarter to a half of the amount used in laboratory SPF tests, so real protection is much lower than the label. Solei counts SPF 30 as about 5.5 times and SPF 50 as about 7 times your unprotected time, and never past the next reapply.

When does it remind me to reapply sunscreen?

Every 2 hours, or every 80 minutes when "swimming / sweating" is on, and right after a swim if you log one.

What is tan mode?

It splits the same safe time between front, back and sides and reminds you to turn. It never adds time. A gradual tan over several days is gentler on skin than one long session, and no tan is risk-free.

I'm not getting notifications.

Solei asks for notification permission the first time you start a timer. If you declined, turn it on in your phone's Settings under Solei (iPhone: Notifications; Android: Notifications, and allow alarms and reminders if asked). Then start a new session.

Can I use it without sharing my location?

Yes. Decline the location prompt and choose a city by hand in Settings.

The app shows an old forecast.

Without a connection, Solei shows the last forecast it saved and how old it is. It refreshes when you open the app online.

What is free and what is Pro?

The UV index, forecast curve, safe sun time, the timer, reapply reminders and tan mode are free for one profile, with no trial period. Pro is optional, as a yearly plan or a one-time lifetime purchase, and adds a morning UV alert, family profiles (up to 6, with a kids preset) and session history. There is no weekly plan.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

Restore: Settings in the app, then Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account. Cancel the yearly plan in your store account: on iPhone, Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play, your profile, Payments and subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

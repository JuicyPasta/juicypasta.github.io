Prefetch privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-22

In short: we collect nothing. Prefetch has no account with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers for it. The app on your device talks directly to Hacker News's services and to the sites whose articles you download. If you sign in to Hacker News, the sign-in cookie stays on your device.

This policy covers the Prefetch app for iOS and Android ("Prefetch", "the app"), made by Jackson Darrow ("we"). Prefetch is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Y Combinator or Hacker News.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server, proxy or relay for the app and receive no data from it. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and has no account or sign-in of ours.

Who the app connects to

To show and download content, the app on your device connects directly to these services. Each of them sees what any website sees when you visit it: your device's IP address, the time, and the request itself. We are not in the middle and see none of it.

  • Hacker News's public API (hacker-news.firebaseio.com, hosted on Google Firebase): story lists, stories, comments and user profiles.
  • The Hacker News search API run by Algolia (hn.algolia.com): whole comment threads, and your search terms when you use search.
  • news.ycombinator.com: only when you are signed in and tap an action (sign in, vote, favorite, reply, flag), or when you open a Hacker News page in the browser view.
  • The sites that stories link to: when the app downloads an article and its images for offline reading, it fetches them from the original site.
  • Apple's App Store or Google Play: for purchases and restoring them.
Downloading ahead of time contacts the linked sites. When Prefetch downloads articles for offline reading, each linked site receives a request from your device, and sees your IP address, as if you had opened that link yourself, including for stories you never go on to read. If you'd rather not, turn off article downloads (or image downloads) in Settings, Offline; the app then contacts a linked site only when you open its article.

Each of these services handles the requests it receives under its own privacy policy: Y Combinator's for Hacker News, Google's for Firebase, Algolia's for search, and each article site's own.

What stays on your device

  • Downloaded content: stories, comments, article reader copies and images, in the app's own storage. It is a personal offline copy; the app does not upload it anywhere, and sharing a story shares the original link, not the copy.
  • What you did: which stories you opened, your saved stories, collapsed threads, hidden stories and users, filters and settings. All of it is stored only on your device.
  • Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google's device backup), if you have it on, includes the small file with your saved list, read marks and settings, like any other app's data. Downloaded content is excluded. That backup is run by Apple or Google under their terms.

Signing in to Hacker News

Signing in is optional. Reading, downloading and saving need no account.

  • Your username and password are sent only to news.ycombinator.com, over HTTPS, exactly as when you sign in on the website. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your password is used once for the sign-in request. It is not stored, logged or written to the app's database.
  • The sign-in cookie that Hacker News returns is stored only on your device, in the system's secure storage (the Keychain on iOS, the Keystore on Android). It is sent only to news.ycombinator.com, and only for actions you tap.
  • Signing out in the app deletes the cookie from your device. Your Hacker News account and anything you posted there are governed by Hacker News, not by this app.

Notifications

If you turn on the "download finished" notification, it is created on your device. No notification service of ours is involved and no push token is collected.

Purchases

Prefetch Pro, where offered, is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns from the store whether Pro is active.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal data. We have none.

Retention, deletion and security

Everything the app stores is on your device, so you decide how long it is kept. Settings, Offline, Free up space removes downloaded content; deleting the app erases everything, the sign-in cookie included. The data is protected by your device's own storage encryption and screen lock. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase.

Children

Prefetch is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Email

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you.

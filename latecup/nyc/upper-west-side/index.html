Coffee shops open late on the Upper West Side

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-25

Latecup lists 18 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés on the Upper West Side that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 5 are open past 10 PM on at least one night, 2 past midnight and 2 are open 24 hours. Prestige Cafe & Market is open 24 hours.

None of these hours has been confirmed by us yet: they come from OpenStreetMap, so check with the shop before a late trip.

How to read this list

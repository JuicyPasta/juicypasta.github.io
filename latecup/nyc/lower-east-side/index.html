Coffee shops open late on the Lower East Side

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-25

Latecup lists 12 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés on the Lower East Side that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 3 are open past 10 PM on at least one night. Joey Bats Café stays open until 12 AM on its latest night.

None of these hours has been confirmed by us yet: they come from OpenStreetMap, so check with the shop before a late trip.

Coffee shops open late in Times Square

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-24

Latecup lists 11 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés in Times Square that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 7 are open past 10 PM on at least one night, 2 past midnight and 1 is open 24 hours. Starbucks is open 24 hours. The latest independent shop: Sugar Factory stays open until 12 AM on its latest night.

None of these hours has been confirmed by us yet: they come from OpenStreetMap, so check with the shop before a late trip.

Coffee shops open late on the Upper East Side

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-25

Latecup lists 15 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés on the Upper East Side that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 3 are open past 10 PM on at least one night, 1 past midnight and 1 is open 24 hours. Dunkin' is open 24 hours. The latest independent shop: Bread N Wine stays open until 11:30 PM on its latest night.

None of these hours has been confirmed by us yet: they come from OpenStreetMap, so check with the shop before a late trip.

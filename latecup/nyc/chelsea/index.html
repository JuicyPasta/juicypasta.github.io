Coffee shops open late in Chelsea

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-24

Latecup lists 27 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés in Chelsea that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 12 are open past 10 PM on at least one night, 3 past midnight and 3 are open 24 hours. Dunkin' is open 24 hours. The latest independent shop: Doughnut Plant stays open until 12 AM on its latest night.

None of these hours has been confirmed by us yet: they come from OpenStreetMap, so check with the shop before a late trip.

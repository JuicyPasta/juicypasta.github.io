How to read this list

  • ✓ Confirmed means we checked the hours against the shop's own website, ordering page or official social account (or, for a chain, its store locator) on the date shown.
  • Unconfirmed hours come from OpenStreetMap, a map kept up by volunteers. The date, when there is one, is when someone last checked them there.
  • Visitors can confirm a place's hours and rate it from 1 to 5 stars on the map. Where they have, this list shows how many confirmed the hours now shown, and the star average with its number of ratings.
  • Seen a wrong time? Open the place on the map and choose "Hours wrong?".

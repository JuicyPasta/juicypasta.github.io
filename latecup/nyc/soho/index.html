Coffee shops open late in SoHo

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-25

Latecup lists 10 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés in SoHo that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 3 are open past 10 PM on at least one night, 1 past midnight and 1 is open 24 hours. Dunkin' is open 24 hours. The latest independent shop: Bibliotheque Cafe & Winebar stays open until 11 PM on its latest night.

We confirmed the hours of 6 of them (marked ✓): 2 from the shop's own site or page and 4 from the chain's store locator. The rest come from OpenStreetMap and are marked unconfirmed, so check before a late trip.

How to read this list

  • ✓ means we confirmed the hours on the date shown, and says from where: "Shop's own site or page" (its website, ordering page or official social account) or "Chain's store locator".
  • Unconfirmed hours come from OpenStreetMap, a map kept up by volunteers. The date, when there is one, is when someone last checked them there.
  • Visitors can confirm a place's hours and rate it from 1 to 5 stars on the map. Where they have, this list shows how many confirmed the hours now shown, and the star average with its number of ratings.
  • Seen a wrong time? Open the place on the map and choose "Hours wrong?".

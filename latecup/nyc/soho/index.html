Coffee shops open late in SoHo

Manhattan, New York City · list updated 2026-11-25

Latecup lists 9 coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés in SoHo that close at 8 PM or later on at least one night: 2 are open past 10 PM on at least one night, 1 past midnight and 1 is open 24 hours. Dunkin' is open 24 hours. The latest independent shop: Bibliotheque Cafe & Winebar stays open until 10 PM on its latest night.

None of these hours has been confirmed by us yet: they come from OpenStreetMap, so check with the shop before a late trip.

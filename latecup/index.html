    It's 9:40 pm. Where can you still get a coffee, and until when?

    Latecup is a map of coffee shops, tea houses and dessert cafés in New York City that stay open late. Other maps tell you what is open now. Latecup answers the next question: what is still open at 10, at 11, at midnight, and what time each place closes. The closing time is written on the pin, and the pin's color shows how much time is left.

    It is free, with no ads and no account. It covers the five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. Browse by area on the neighborhood pages, for example late coffee in the East Village, Koreatown or Astoria.

    The apps for iPhone, iPad and Android are on their way. Until they are in the stores, this page is the whole product.

    How the map decides what "late" means

    • New York time. Everything is worked out on New York's clock, wherever you are reading from.
    • A night belongs to the day it started on. At 1:30 am on Saturday the map is still showing Friday night. The night rolls over at 5 am.
    • The chips. Open now shows what is open this minute. 8 PM+, 9 PM+, 10 PM+, 11 PM+, Midnight+ and All night show the places that are open at that time tonight. A shop that closes at 10 is not counted as open at 10. All night means open at 3 am. A time that has already passed drops off the row.
    • "24 hours" is only for a place that is open round the clock and stays open through the coming night. On the last night of a 24-hour run, a shop that shuts at midnight says midnight.
    • Color. Amber: two hours or more left. Orange: under two hours. Red-orange: closing within the hour.
    • Another night. Use the night picker to check any of the next seven nights.

    Where the hours come from, and how sure they are

    Each place says where its hours come from, and the two kinds of confirmation are never mixed:

    • Confirmed by us. Each place says which of two sources we used and the date: "from the shop's own site or page" (its website, its ordering page or its official social account), or "from the chain's store locator". Most confirmed chain shops come from store locators.
    • Confirmed by visitors. People who use Latecup can press Hours are right on a place. The place then says how many visitors confirmed its hours and when the latest did. A confirmation is for the hours shown that day: if the hours change, the count starts again.
    • Unconfirmed. The hours come from OpenStreetMap, a map that volunteers keep up. They are often right and sometimes out of date. Check with the shop before a late trip.

    The ✓ in the list, and the "Confirmed hours only" box, mean confirmed by us or by two or more visitors. If someone reports the hours as wrong after the latest confirmation, the place says "Reported wrong: being checked" and loses its ✓ until we have looked.

    On holidays the map shows a notice: shops change their hours and we never guess holiday hours. Any shop can close early without notice. When a late trip matters, call or check the shop's own page.

    Help

    Need help? Email dangochains@googlegroups.com with "Latecup" in the subject. That's the developer's support address for all his apps. For a problem in the app, please include your device and what you expected to see.

    A place has the wrong hours. How do I report it?

    Open the place (tap its pin or its row in the list) and choose Hours wrong? Pick what is wrong, add the right hours if you know them, and send. In the app it is the same button on the place screen. A report contains the place, your choice, what you typed and the app version. Each report is checked against the shop's own site or page, or the chain's store locator, and the map is corrected in a daily update.

    How do I confirm hours or rate a café?

    Open the place. Hours are right confirms the hours you are looking at, once per place per day. The stars rate the café as a place to be late, from 1 to 5; tap again to change your rating. You don't need an account. Your own answers show at once on your device, and the totals that everybody sees are recounted about once an hour. Ratings are stars only: there are no written reviews.

    A confirmation or rating sends the place, your answer, the app version and a random ID made by the app or by this page, so that one person counts once. It sends nothing about where you are, and you don't have to be at the café. The random ID isn't linked to your name, an account or your device. The privacy policy has the detail.

    A late coffee shop is missing.

    A place is on the map only when we know its hours and it closes at 8 PM or later on at least one night. If a shop you know is missing, email its name and street to the address above. If you edit OpenStreetMap, adding the shop's opening_hours there fixes it for everybody.

    I run a shop and want my listing fixed or removed.

    Email the address above from the shop's own address, or send a link to the page that shows your hours. We update or remove the listing.

    Why does it only cover New York City?

    Confirming hours takes work, so Latecup starts with one city. Tell us which city you want.

    Want Latecup in your city?

    Sends the city name you type and this page's version ("web 1"), nothing else.

    Does the map know where I am?

    Only if you press Near me, and then your location stays in this page to sort the list by distance. It is never sent to us or anyone else. A confirmation or a rating says nothing about where you are. See the privacy policy.

    What do I need to use the app?

    An iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later, or an Android phone or tablet with Android 7 or later, once the app is in each store. The list, search and saved places keep working without a connection; the map background needs one.

    Delete your data

    Latecup has no account, so there is very little to delete, and most of it is on your own device.

    • On your device. Saved places, settings, your own confirmations and ratings and the random ID live in the app. Deleting the app removes them (if your phone's own backup restores the app's data, they can come back with it). On the web map they live in this browser: the button below removes them, and so does clearing this site's data.
    • What you sent us. Reports ("Hours wrong?", a city request), confirmations ("Hours are right") and star ratings are kept until you ask us to remove them. Email dangochains@googlegroups.com with "Latecup delete my data" in the subject and either your random ID, or the place and the day and time you sent it. We remove the matching entries within 30 days, and the public counts and star averages are recounted without them within about an hour of that.
    • Your random ID. It is what lets us find your confirmations and ratings; nothing else ties them to you. On the web map: · . If you can't find your ID, the place and the time are enough for us to look.

    Nothing we hold says who you are: no name, no email address, no location, no device identifier. A confirmation older than 120 days stops being counted whether or not you ask. The privacy policy lists what each kind of message contains.

Latecup privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-24

In short: Latecup has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location is used on your device to show where you are and how far each place is, and it is never sent anywhere. The app downloads a public list of places that is the same for everyone, and map tiles for the area you look at. If you choose to report wrong hours, the report contains only what you typed, the place, and the app version.

This policy covers the Latecup app for iPhone and iPad and the Latecup web map at juicypasta.github.io/latecup ("Latecup"), made by Jackson Darrow ("we").

What we collect

Nothing, unless you send a report (below). Latecup has no account or sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. The web map sets no cookies. We receive no information about you or about how you use Latecup.

Location

The app asks for your location while you use it, and the web map asks only if you press "Near me". Your location is used on your device to draw your dot on the map, to show the distance to each place and to sort the list. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. Latecup works without it: say no and the map opens on the city centre.

What Latecup downloads

  • The list of places. Latecup downloads the list of places and their hours from a public website we publish on GitHub Pages (juicypasta.github.io/latecup-data). The files are identical for every user. The request does not contain your location, your saved places, an identifier or any other user data: everything you see is worked out on your device.
  • Map tiles. The map background comes from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Like any online map, the request names the area of the map on your screen. It does not contain your location or an identifier.

As with any internet request, the servers that answer (GitHub for the list of places and this website, OpenFreeMap for map tiles) see your device's IP address and the usual technical details of a web request. We have no access to those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement and OpenFreeMap's privacy policy.

Reports you choose to send

"Hours wrong?" and "Want Latecup in your city?" send a report only when you press Send. A report contains:

  • the kind of problem you picked,
  • the place it is about (its name and its public ID in our list),
  • the note or city name you typed, if any,
  • the app version (for example "ios 1.0.0" or "web 1").

It does not contain your location, your name, your email address, a device identifier or your saved places. Reports are received through a Google Form that does not ask for a sign-in and does not collect email addresses; Google processes the submission under its privacy policy and, as with any web request, sees your IP address. We use reports only to correct the list of places and to decide which cities to add. Please don't put personal information in the note. To have a report deleted, email us and describe it.

What stays on your device

Your saved places, your settings and the last downloaded list of places are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be included in your device's own backup (iCloud), which is governed by Apple's terms. The web map stores nothing in your browser.

Links that leave Latecup

Directions, Call, Website, Instagram and Share open another app or website that you choose. Directions passes the place's coordinates to your maps app. What those apps and sites do is covered by their own policies.

Purchases

Latecup is free and has no purchases.

Children

Latecup is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give access to, correct or delete, apart from a report you sent, which contains no identity. You can turn location off for Latecup at any time in your device's Settings, and remove saved places in the app.

Changes

If Latecup's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version is released.

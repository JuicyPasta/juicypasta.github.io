Latecup privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-24

In short: Latecup has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location is used on your device to show where you are and how far each place is, and it is never sent anywhere. The app downloads a public list of places that is the same for everyone, and map tiles for the area you look at. If you choose to report wrong hours, the report contains the kind of problem you picked, what you typed, the place, and the app version. If you choose to confirm a place's hours or rate it, that answer is sent with the place, the app version and a random ID so that one person counts once. It says nothing about where you are, and it is not linked to your name, an account or your device.

This policy covers the Latecup app for iPhone, iPad and Android and the Latecup web map at juicypasta.github.io/latecup ("Latecup"), made by Jackson Darrow ("we").

What we collect

Nothing, unless you send a report, a confirmation or a rating (both below). Latecup has no account or sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. The web map sets no cookies. We receive no information about who you are or about how you use Latecup.

Location

The app asks for your location while you use it, and the web map asks only if you press "Near me". Your location is used on your device to draw your dot on the map, to show the distance to each place and to sort the list. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. Latecup works without it: say no and the map opens on the city center.

What Latecup downloads

  • The list of places. Latecup downloads the list of places and their hours from a public website we publish on GitHub Pages (juicypasta.github.io/latecup-data). The files are identical for every user. The request does not contain your location, your saved places, an identifier or any other user data: everything you see is worked out on your device.
  • Map tiles. The map background comes from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Like any online map, the request names the area of the map on your screen. It does not contain your location or an identifier.

As with any internet request, the servers that answer (GitHub for the list of places and this website, OpenFreeMap for map tiles) see your device's IP address and the usual technical details of a web request. We have no access to those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement and OpenFreeMap's privacy policy.

Reports you choose to send

"Hours wrong?" and "Want Latecup in your city?" send a report only when you press Send. A report contains:

  • the kind of problem you picked,
  • the place it is about (its name and its public ID in our list),
  • the note or city name you typed, if any,
  • the app version (for example "ios 1.0.0" or "web 1").

A report does not contain your location, your name, your email address, a device identifier, the random ID described below or your saved places. Reports are received through a Google Form that does not ask for a sign-in and does not collect email addresses; Google processes the submission under its privacy policy and, as with any web request, sees your IP address. We use reports only to correct the list of places and to decide which cities to add. Please don't put personal information in the note. To have a report deleted, email us and describe it.

Confirmations and ratings you choose to send

On a place you can press "Hours are right" to confirm the hours shown, and you can rate the café from 1 to 5 stars. Each is sent only when you press it, and each sends four things and nothing else:

  • the place (its name and its public ID in our list);
  • your answer: either "these hours are right", together with a short code that identifies the hours table you were looking at, or your number of stars. Ratings are stars only; there is no text;
  • the app version (for example "ios 1.0.0" or "web 1");
  • a random ID. The app makes it the first time you confirm or rate and keeps it on your device; the web map makes its own the first time you confirm or rate there, and keeps it in your browser. It exists so that one person counts once per place.

Nothing about where you are is sent. A confirmation or rating carries no location and no coordinates, and it does not say whether you were at the café. You don't have to be there to confirm or rate, and Latecup does not check.

The random ID is not your name, your account, your email address, your phone number, an advertising ID or an identifier of your device. It is not linked to any of those, and we have no way to tell who it belongs to. It is not used for anything except counting, and it is not shared with anyone for advertising or tracking. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it from your device; the next confirmation makes a new one. One exception: if your phone restores the app's data from its own backup (Android does this by itself when you reinstall an app on the same Google account), the ID can come back with it.

These answers go through the same Google Form as reports (no sign-in, no email collected; Google sees your IP address as with any web request). About once an hour they are counted, and only the totals are published: how many visitors confirmed a place's hours and when the latest did, and the star average with its number of ratings. The random IDs are never shown in the app or on this site. To have a confirmation or rating removed, see Delete your data.

What stays on your device

Your saved places, your settings, your own confirmations and ratings, the random ID and the last downloaded list of places are stored on your device. Saved places and settings are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it. They may be included in your device's own backup (iCloud on Apple devices, Google's backup on Android), which is governed by Apple's or Google's terms. The web map stores nothing in your browser until you confirm or rate. From then on it keeps two things in your browser's local storage: the random ID and your own confirmations and ratings, so that it can show them to you and count you once. It sets no cookies.

Links that leave Latecup

Directions, Call, Website, Instagram and Share open another app or website that you choose. Directions passes the place's coordinates to your maps app. What those apps and sites do is covered by their own policies.

Purchases

Latecup is free and has no purchases.

Children

Latecup is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give access to, correct or delete, apart from the reports, confirmations and ratings you sent, which carry no identity. You can turn location off for Latecup at any time in your device's Settings, and remove saved places in the app. How to have what you sent removed: Delete your data.

Changes

If Latecup's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version is released.

Solray privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-22

In short: Solray has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings and history stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is your position, rounded to about 1 km, sent to the Norwegian weather service to fetch the UV forecast.

This policy covers the Solray app for iOS and Android ("Solray", "the app"), made by Jackson Darrow ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers for the app and receive no data from it. The app has no account or sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Location

  • If you allow it, the app reads your phone's location to show the UV forecast for where you are. If you decline, you can choose a city by hand; your phone's own system looks up the city's coordinates.
  • Your location is used on your device. To fetch the forecast, the app rounds the coordinates to two decimal places (about 1 km) and sends them over an encrypted connection to MET Norway, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, at api.met.no.
  • The location is sent nowhere else, and never to us.
  • As with any internet request, MET Norway's servers see your device's IP address. How MET Norway handles requests is described in its privacy policy.
  • The app does not track your location in the background.

What stays on your device

Your skin type, usual SPF, profiles, settings, the saved forecast and your session history are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be included in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google), which is governed by Apple's or Google's terms.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service of ours is involved and no push token is collected.

Purchases

Solray Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns from the store whether Pro is active.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal data. There is none to sell or share. The only transfer is the rounded location sent to MET Norway, described above.

Children

Solray is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included. A kids profile, if a parent sets one up, is stored only on the parent's device.

Your choices

  • Turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings and use a city instead.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase.

Not medical advice

Solray shows estimates for general sun-safety information. It is not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
